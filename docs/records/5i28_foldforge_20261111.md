# 5I28, FoldForge against the Anthropic kits (2026-11-11)

The first FoldForge record of the [5I28 benchmark](../guides/BENCHMARK-5I28.md): eight models on miniworld-engine 2.5.1, one H100 80GB
each, against the kit measurements of the same day. Data: [data/5i28_foldforge_20261111.csv](data/5i28_foldforge_20261111.csv) (every
cell, all warm forwards, the kit source of each number). This file is not edited afterwards.

## Conditions

- **FoldForge:** run `run_1111b` (`benchmarks/5i28_ff/`), node02, 2026-11-11 17:46-17:55 KST, `NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3`, torch 2.13.0+cu129,
  miniworld-engine and kernels 2.5.1. Config: `backend: miniworld`, `precision: bf16`, `mode: exact`, 4 trunk passes (AF3: `recycles` 3),
  200 steps, 5 samples, `compile` + CUDA graphs (`scope: trunk`), bucketing on, 3 warm repeats per cell. Real MSA (P0113359, 6790 rows),
  4 distinct real templates (RoseTTAFold3: one per chain; ESMFold2: none). One process per model, n ascending.
- **Code:** the run's `meta.json` names `6bbca72` with a dirty tree; the tree was `6bbca72` plus the changes committed as `8d02a8f`
  (fp32 activations to the autocast dtype at the engine's master modules), `bdb601a` (RF3 template edges, CUDA-graph safe) and `ff2df11`
  (ESM-C checkpoint layout), and the ESMFold2 no-template line of `248c51b`. The runner's per-size memory release (`248c51b`) came after
  the run: ESMFold2 n=16 here is the runner's expandable-segments retry in a fresh process (15.285 s); the release, in one process,
  gives 15.168 s.
- **Kits:** `~/miniworld-engine-scratch/bench5i28_1111` (`done_final/`, `done_sep/`, authority in its `NOTES_authoritative.md`), the
  same inputs and settings, one GPU per job. Number: each kit's forward span (`fwd`), median of the warm forwards.

## Notes

- **ESMFold2 is the one model slower than its kit** (0.57x at n=1, rising to parity at n=12). Not analysed in this run.
- **Not measured on FoldForge:** Chai-1 (every n) and OpenDDE n >= 8. Both are engine refusals that raise instead of refusing
  (`ValueError` from the Hopper token CUDA candidate of `conditioned_transition`; `RuntimeError` "q / k / v ... fp16 rows at one common
  stride" from the TriAttn h16 sm90 kernel), so the pytorch fallback never runs. The engine side is changing them to
  `BackendUnsupported`; the cells are to be measured on that release.
- **Still on the pytorch backend in these numbers** (refusals, not failures): `MSAPairWeightedAveraging` at N > 1024 (n = 12, 16),
  and `AttentionPairBias` in the confidence head batched over the samples (B > 1).
- **Kit cells:** Chai-1's kit reports `exit=1` on every cell after its forwards ran (its numbers are those forwards). Boltz-2 and
  ESMFold2 n=16 ran out of memory in the kit, OpenDDE n=16 failed. RoseTTAFold3 uses one template (`templates_real=1/1`) on both sides.
- **What changed against the earlier run of the day (`run_1111`):** that run is void. Its confidence head (all models), template
  embedder and atom transformer handed fp32 activations to the engine, whose modules then ran their fp32 pytorch backends (the
  out-of-memory cells at n >= 8); RoseTTAFold3 failed its trunk capture at n <= 4; ESMFold2 folded on a randomly initialised ESM-C
  tower (the checkpoint layout was not read since 2026-10-19). All three are fixed in the commits above.
- Node: node02, 7 of 8 jobs started with CPULoad 0.00; the training job of the day ran on node01.
